# Homeroom — first-time setup

Everything here lives under the **Setup** tab. Changes save automatically
as you type — there's no separate Save button anywhere in Setup.

Also published as a formatted page: see the Artifact link shared alongside
this file, or ask to have it republished if the link's gone stale.

---

## Do this once

### 1. School info (Setup → School info)
Name, reporting authority, administrator, principal, county, school ID,
address, phone. This fills in the header of every printed report card
and transcript — worth getting right before you print anything official.

### 2. Grading scale (Setup → Grading scale)
The letter-grade bands and their GPA values (e.g. A ≥ 90% → 4.0). A score
gets the highest band whose minimum percent it meets or beats. This one
scale is used everywhere — report cards, transcript, GPA. Check it
matches what you actually want before you start entering grades; it's
easy to change later, but past grades will re-letter themselves against
whatever the scale currently says.

### 3. Students (Setup → Students)
Add each learner: name, a color (click the swatch to change it — used
throughout the app to tell them apart at a glance), and optionally a
date of birth (only shown on the transcript header; nothing is
calculated from it).

---

## Do this at the start of every school year

### 4. Create the year (Setup → Years)
Add a year with a label (e.g. `2027-2028`) and a type:
- **Tracked** — the current year you're actively planning and grading
  lesson-by-lesson, day to day.
- **Historical** — a completed year where you're just recording final
  course grades, not individual assignments.

Click **Set active** on the new year. The active year is what Planner,
Grades, Calendar, and the Dashboard all show — last year's data doesn't
go anywhere, it's still there and still shows up on the Transcript, but
day-to-day work happens in whichever year is active.

While you're on this tab, set each student's **grade level** for the new
year (e.g. "11th Grade") — it's per-student, per-year, so nothing is
inferred from age or from last year's grade automatically.

### 5. Quarters (Setup → Quarters)
Add the terms for the active year (typically four) with a name and
start/end dates. The dates are just for your reference — you pick the
quarter directly when you plan or grade a lesson, so nothing depends on
today's date falling inside the right range.

### 6. Subjects (Setup → Subjects)
Per student, per year. Each subject can carry a category (e.g. "Math"),
credit hours (defaults to 1, for the transcript), and optionally a
target count + unit label (e.g. "36 lessons") if you want the Report
view to show progress toward a total.

If a student had subjects last year, Homeroom offers a **carry forward**
shortcut at the top of their list — check the ones that continue into
the new year and it copies them over (name, category, credit hours) so
you're not retyping "Algebra II" from scratch.

## You're set up — now what?

- **Tracked year:** head to **Planner** to start adding lessons, or use
  **Planner → Import** to paste in a whole scope-and-sequence at once
  (see `HOW-TO.md`).
- **Historical year:** head to **Transcript** to enter that year's final
  course grades — one at a time, or **Bulk import** a whole year at once
  (see `HOW-TO.md`).
- For how the app is actually used week to week once it's set up, see
  `DAILY-USE.md`.
- **Back up once you've got real data in** — Setup → Data → Export.
  There's no cloud copy; see `HOW-TO.md` for the full backup/restore
  story.
